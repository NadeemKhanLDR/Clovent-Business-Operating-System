## 1. Purpose of Product Decision Records

Product Decision Records (PDRs) document durable business, product, and financial policies governing the Clovent Business Operating System. While Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) document technical implementation choices, PDRs capture product boundaries, financial rules, and commercial commitments.
