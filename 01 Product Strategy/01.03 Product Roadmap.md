# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

Define the planned evolution of Clovent Business Operating System.

---

# Phase 1 - Foundation Platform

## Objective

Build the reusable platform that every business module depends on.

### Deliverables

- Authentication
- Authorization
- Organization Management
- Company Management
- Branch Management
- User Management
- Role Management
- Settings
- Audit Logs
- Notification Engine
- File Management
- Reporting Framework
- Plugin Framework
- AI Infrastructure

---

# Phase 2 - Restaurant POS (MVP)

## Objective

Release the first commercial product.

### Deliverables

- Menu Management
- Table Management
- Order Management
- Kitchen Display
- Billing
- Payments
- Discounts
- Taxes
- Receipts
- Shift Management
- Inventory Integration
- Customer Management
- Basic Reports
