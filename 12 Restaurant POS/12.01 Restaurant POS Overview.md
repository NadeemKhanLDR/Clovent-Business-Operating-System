## B. Business Rules

### 1. Item Addition & Variant Naming
- **Single Variant Products:** Clicking a menu item adds 1x Standard portion variant to the cart.
- **Multi-Variant Products (Full / Half):** Each variant displays as `[Product Name] - [Portion]` (e.g. `Aloo Gobi - Full`, `Aloo Gobi - Half`).
- **Naming Consistency:** The `Product Name - Portion` naming format is used consistently across Cart, Hold, Recall, Active Orders, Receipts, Payment History, and Sales History.
- **Portion Normalization:** "Regular" or default portions render as `Product Name` without redundant portion suffixes.

### 2. Duplicate Item Behavior
- **Same Item + Same Variant + No Notes:** Merges into a single line with incremented quantity (`Quantity = Quantity + addedQty`).
- **Same Item + Same Variant + Custom Notes:** Maintained as separate lines to ensure distinct kitchen modifiers (e.g., `Note: Less spicy` vs. `Note: Extra spicy`) do not overwrite one another.

### 3. Quantity Controls
- **Validation:** Quantity must be a positive integer ($\ge 1$).
- **Controls:** `+` (increment), `-` (decrement), and `Qty` button / inline cell editor.
- **Immediate Recalculation:** Changing quantity immediately recalculates Subtotal, Tax, Discount, Service Charge, Total Payable, and Balance Due across all UI controls and domain models.

### 4. Notes Persistence
- **Item Notes (`OrderLine.Notes`):** Attached per cart line. Persisted in database and preserved across `MENU -> CART -> HOLD -> RECALL -> CART -> COMPLETE`.
- **Order Notes (`Order.Notes`):** Free-text internal notes attached to the order.
- **Customer Notes (`Order.CustomerNotes`):** Customer-facing notes printed on receipts.

### 5. Delete vs. Void Semantics
- **Delete (`RemoveOrderLineCommand`):** Removes uncommitted lines from an active working order before payment/kitchen processing.
- **Void (`VoidOrderLineCommand` / `VoidOrderCommand`):** Audit operation. Sets `IsVoided = true`. Voided lines and orders are excluded from financial totals and kitchen tickets but preserved permanently in the database for audit trails.
- **Manager Authorization:** Full order voiding requires reason prompt and Manager Authorization (`pos.void` permission).

### 6. Price Override
- **Catalog Snapshot:** `OriginalUnitPrice` preserves the catalog price at creation.
- **Price Edit (`OverrideOrderLinePriceCommand`):** Authorized via `pos.priceoverride` permission (button or inline grid editor). Requires a reason and records the cashier/manager user.
- **Persistence:** Overridden prices persist through Hold, Recall, Receipt printing, and Sales History.

### 7. Authoritative Total Calculation
All money totals are computed by `OrderTotalsCalculator` via a single, authoritative path:
$$\text{Subtotal} = \sum (\text{Quantity} \times \text{UnitPrice} \quad \forall \text{ active lines})$$
$$\text{Grand Total} = \text{Subtotal} - \text{DiscountTotal} + \text{ServiceChargeTotal} + \text{ExclusiveTaxTotal}$$
$$\text{Balance Due} = \text{Grand Total} - \text{PaidTotal}$$

### 8. Balance Due Behavior
- **Dynamic Balance:** `Balance Due` equals `Grand Total` minus `PaidTotal` (non-voided payments).
- **Partial Payments:** Subtotal and Grand Total remain fixed while Balance Due decreases as partial payments are recorded.

### 9. Hold & Recall Integration
- **Hold Order (`HoldOrderCommand`):** Transitions order status to `Held`. Preserves order ID, number, items, variants, quantities, notes, prices, customer, and table. Clears the working cart UI safely without deleting persisted orders.
- **Recall Order (`RecallOrderDialog`):** Restores held orders back into the POS workspace with 100% data fidelity.
- **Safety Checks:** Switching orders or recalling a held order when active unsaved edits exist prompts the cashier to hold the current order first.

### 10. Clear Working Cart
- **Clear Action (`ClearOrderAsync`):** Safely resets the UI workspace (`_currentOrder = null`, `_currentOrderLines = []`) without deleting or cancelling held or open orders in the database.

### 11. Dine In vs. Take Away
- **Dine In:** Requires a valid table (`TableId`). Table selector control is enabled.
- **Take Away:** `TableId` is `null`. Table selector control is disabled and unselected.

### 12. Customer & Table Association
- **Customer Association:** Defaults to Walk-in Customer (`CustomerId = null`). Selecting a customer associates them with `Order.CustomerId` and displays outstanding balance.
- **Table Association:** Occupied tables link to `Order.TableId` and update occupancy status on the table map. Vacated when order is completed or voided.

## C. Order Lifecycle Workflow

$$\text{NEW} \longrightarrow \text{ADD ITEMS} \longrightarrow \text{MODIFY CART} \longrightarrow \text{HOLD} \longrightarrow \text{CLEAR UI} \longrightarrow \text{RECALL} \longrightarrow \text{RECORD PAYMENT} \longrightarrow \text{COMPLETE}$$

- **Open:** Active order receiving item additions or modifications.
- **Held:** Persisted in database, available for recall.
- **Completed:** Fully paid ($\text{Balance} \le 0.005$), inventory stock issued, table vacated.
- **Voided:** Discarded with audit reason and manager authorization.
- **Cancelled:** Cancelled before any payment has been recorded.

## I. Shift Management & Cash Register Balancing

### 1. Business Purpose & Scope
Shift Management establishes complete cash register drawer session lifecycle tracking, blind cash count reconciliation, cash movement (in/out) auditing, and shift history oversight without modifying or interrupting core Restaurant POS order entry.

### 2. Architecture & Domain Rules
- **Shift Aggregate Root (`Shift`):** Manages shift session state (`Open`, `Closed`), starting cash float, expected cash, counted cash, variance, and notes.
- **Single Active Shift Guard:** A terminal and a cashier may each have at most ONE open shift session at any given time.
- **POS Active Shift Check:** `RestaurantPosForm` ensures an active shift exists for the active terminal before allowing cash payment recording.
- **Cash Movement Entity (`CashMovement`):** Records drawer deposits (`CashIn`) and drops (`CashOut`) with user attribution, amount, and business reason.
- **Financial Calculation:**
  $$\text{Expected Cash} = \text{Starting Cash} + \text{CashIn} - \text{CashOut} + \text{Cash Sales}$$
- **Blind Cash Count:** At shift closure (`CloseShiftDialog`), cashiers enter actual counted cash without seeing system expected cash.
- **Variance Audit:** Monetary variance ($\text{Counted Cash} - \text{Expected Cash}$) requires an explicit business explanation if non-zero.
- **Shift History & Detail (`ShiftHistoryView`, `ShiftDetailDialog`):** Full audit reporting grid with date range and status filtering.

## J. POS PIN Login

### 1. Purpose
Cashiers sign in to the Restaurant POS with only a PIN — no username or password — while username/password login (and Back Office) continue to work unchanged.

### 2. Configuration Location
**Back Office → Administration → Users → (select user) → Set PIN / Clear PIN** (`UsersForm` toolbar, feature-gated by `feature.users.setpin`). The PIN entry dialog is `PinPromptForm` (New PIN + Confirm PIN, masked). The stored PIN is never displayed after saving; changing a PIN is always a fresh assignment, and Clear PIN removes the credential entirely (a user cannot be left with a guessable "empty" PIN).

### 3. Security / Storage
- PINs are hashed with the existing Authentication hashing infrastructure (`IPinHasher` / `Pbkdf2PinHasher`, PBKDF2 — same primitive as passwords). Only the hash is stored, in the existing `Authentication.UserCredentials.PinHash` column (nvarchar(512), nullable) — **no database schema change was required**.
- `PinPolicy.Default` enforces 4–6 digits, rejecting repeated ("1111") and sequential ("1234") PINs.
- PINs are **unique across users**: PIN-only sign-in resolves the user FROM the PIN, so a duplicate would make authentication ambiguous. Because PBKDF2 hashes are salted there is no indexable lookup value; uniqueness is enforced by `SetPinCommandHandler` verifying the candidate against every other user's stored PIN hash before assignment.
- Every PIN set/clear rotates the `SecurityStamp` (domain events `PinChanged`/`PinCleared`).

### 4. Authentication Flow
1. `LoginForm`: Username is no longer required when a PIN is entered.
2. `LoginService.LoginAsync` detects PIN-only input (blank username + PIN) and resolves the user by verifying the PIN against every stored PIN hash (`ResolveUserByPinAsync`).
3. The resolved user then passes through the SAME status checks (locked/inactive), audit records (`RecordLoginAttemptCommand`, `RecordCredentialCheckCommand`), session start (`StartSessionCommand`), and post-login module permission check (`menu.pos`) as a username/password login. Invalid PINs get the same generic "Invalid username or password." message — the UI never reveals whether a PIN is in use.

### 5. Session, Permissions & Shift Integration
PIN login establishes the identical `ICurrentSession` (UserId/SessionId/DisplayName) that username/password login would establish for the same user — there is no special "PIN user". Permissions are evaluated normally (module visibility, POS feature gates), and Shift Management therefore sees the correct cashier, because shift cashier identity is derived from the signed-in session user. PIN authentication does not bypass any permission, void, price-override, or payment authorization rule.

### 6. Automated Verification (application-level, NOT live UI)
`Clovent.Authentication.Application.Tests/Credentials/PinCommandTests.cs` (set/clear/policy/duplicate/no-plaintext) and `Clovent.Desktop.Tests/Login/LoginServiceTests.cs` (PIN-only resolution, unknown PIN, inactive user, multi-user disambiguation). These are automated integration tests — they do not by themselves prove the WinForms UI works.

### 7. Limitations / Technical Debt
- PIN resolution verifies every stored PIN hash (O(users) PBKDF2 verifications per PIN-only login) — acceptable at restaurant-scale user counts; a keyed HMAC lookup column is the scalability path if ever needed.
- Uniqueness is enforced in the application layer, not a database constraint (a consequence of salted hashing).
- `ManagerAuthorizationService` (feature-code re-auth prompts) still accepts password only.

## K. Table Occupancy Lifecycle & State Synchronization

### 1. Purpose
Defines the authoritative business rules and technical mechanisms governing real-time table occupancy status (`Available`, `Occupied`, `Reserved`, `OutOfService`) across order creation, cancellation, voiding, completion, hold/recall, application restart, and POS UI table selection.

### 2. Table Occupancy Business Rules
- **Active Seating:** A table is `Occupied` if and only if there is at least one active (`Open` or `Held`) Dine-In order assigned to that table.
- **Order Cancellation:** Cancelling a Dine-In order (`CancelOrderCommand`) releases table occupancy to `Available` if no other active (`Open` or `Held`) order is seated at that table.
- **Order Voiding:** Voiding a Dine-In order (`VoidOrderCommand`) releases table occupancy to `Available` if no other active order remains on that table.
- **Order Completion:** Completing a paid Dine-In order (`CompleteOrderCommand`) releases table occupancy to `Available` if no other active order remains.
- **Take Away Orders:** Take Away orders carry no table association (`TableId = null`) and never occupy any table.
- **Held Orders:** A Held Dine-In order represents a seated party and legitimately maintains table occupancy as `Occupied`.
- **Multi-Order Protection:** If multiple orders exist for a single table (e.g. historical/split data), completing or cancelling one order checks for remaining active orders on `TableId` before vacating the table, preserving `Occupied` state if another active order exists.

### 3. UI Synchronization & Persistence
- After cancelling or voiding an order in `RestaurantPosForm`, `await ReloadTablesAsync()` is invoked to fetch updated table statuses from the database via `ListAllTablesQuery`.
- `TablePickerEdit` (`_tablePicker`) updates its internal items list and display strings immediately so table availability (`T-03 (Available)`) is reflected without needing application restart.
- Occupancy changes are transactionally committed to the database per MediatR handler unit of work (`UnitOfWorkBehavior`).

### 4. Verification
- **Automated Tests:** Covered in `Clovent.Restaurant.Application.Tests/Orders/OrderHandlerTests.cs` (`CancelOrderCommandHandler_DineIn_VacatesTable`, `CancelOrderCommandHandler_DineIn_KeepsTableOccupied_WhenAnotherActiveOrderExists`, `VoidOrderCommandHandler_DineIn_KeepsTableOccupied_WhenAnotherActiveOrderExists`, `TakeAwayOrder_DoesNotOccupyTable`, `ReopenOrderCommandHandler_DineIn_ReoccupiesTable`).

## L. POS Menu Item Eligibility & Category Count Reconciliation

### 1. Purpose
Defines the canonical eligibility rules for displaying menu items in the POS tile grid and calculating tab counts for **All Menu** and individual category tabs.

### 2. POS Menu Item Eligibility Rules
A menu item is eligible for display in the POS if and only if:
- The `ProductVariant.Status` equals `"Active"`.
- The parent `Product.Status` equals `"Active"`.
- The `Product` is not deleted/soft-deleted.

### 3. Category Count & Reconciliation Rules
- **All Menu Count:** Calculated as `COUNT(DISTINCT ProductId)` across all eligible active variants.
- **Category Tab Counts:** Calculated as `COUNT(DISTINCT ProductId)` of eligible active variants belonging to each active category.
- **Uncategorized Handling:** Any eligible active variant where `ProductCategoryId` is `null` or points to an inactive/deleted category is rendered under an explicit **Uncategorized** category tab (`🏷️ Uncategorized`).
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{All Menu Count} = \sum_{c \in \text{Active Categories}} \text{Count}(c) + \text{Count}(\text{Uncategorized})$$
- This rule guarantees that every single item listed in **All Menu** is fully accounted for by the visible category tabs without any discrepancy.
